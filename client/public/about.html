About BookAFriend

Good company for every plan.

BookAFriend is a marketplace designed to help adults find platonic companionship for everyday activities and social occasions. Whether someone wants company for shopping, movies, events, studying, games, walks or an eligible caregiving service, BookAFriend aims to make discovering and arranging that company straightforward.

Our purpose

We believe people should have more opportunities to enjoy activities with good company. BookAFriend provides a platform where eligible adults can discover companion profiles, review listed services and rates, and request bookings.

How BookAFriend works

  1. Create an account. Members provide their details and complete the required identity-review process.
  2. Discover companions. Eligible members can browse available profiles, services, rates and availability.
  3. Request a booking. Select a service, date, time and permitted venue, then submit a booking request.
  4. Pay through the platform. Payments are processed through the payment provider made available at checkout. Booking status and applicable charges are shown in the app.
  5. Meet respectfully and safely. Follow the platform rules, respect personal boundaries and use the reporting tools if a concern arises.

Platonic companionship only

BookAFriend is intended for platonic companionship. It is not a dating, escort, matrimonial or sexual-services platform. Members must respect one another's consent, privacy and boundaries. Harassment, coercion, impersonation, illegal activity and attempts to arrange prohibited services are not permitted.

Independent members

Companions who list services on BookAFriend are independent members, not employees of BookAFriend. They are responsible for the services they offer, subject to applicable law and the platform's Terms of Use. BookAFriend provides the marketplace and related platform features.

Safety and verification

We provide an account-review process and features intended to support safer use, including booking controls, reporting and blocking tools, and SOS notifications. These measures have limitations and cannot guarantee a person's identity, behaviour or safety at a meeting.

Choose an appropriate public or registered venue, tell someone you trust about your plans, and leave if you feel uncomfortable. In an emergency in India, call 112. Do not rely on an in-app report or SOS notification as a substitute for emergency services.

Payments and support

Booking payments are handled through the payment provider offered in the app. Fees, cancellation conditions and refund eligibility are described in our policies and displayed where applicable.

For account, booking, safety or policy questions, visit our Contact Us page.
