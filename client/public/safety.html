Community & Safety Guidelines

For every BookAFriend member

BookAFriend is a marketplace for platonic companionship. These guidelines explain the conduct expected from members and provide practical tips for using the platform more safely. They supplement, and do not replace, our Terms of Use.

1. Who can use BookAFriend

  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • Provide accurate account information and complete the required review process.
  • Do not impersonate another person or upload forged or misleading documents.
  • Keep your password and login credentials private.

2. Respect and consent

  • Treat other members respectfully and communicate clearly.
  • Respect personal boundaries. Consent can be withdrawn at any time.
  • Do not threaten, harass, stalk, discriminate against, pressure or intimidate another member.
  • Do not share another person's private details, images or messages without appropriate permission.
  • BookAFriend is strictly platonic. Sexual services, unwanted romantic or sexual pressure, and physical intimacy are prohibited.

3. Bookings and venues

  • Keep bookings and payments within the platform.
  • Use the permitted venue types for the selected service.
  • Do not pressure a companion to stay beyond the booked time or perform a service they have not agreed to.
  • Additional time must be requested and paid for through the app where that feature is available.
  • Either person may end an interaction if they feel unsafe or uncomfortable.

4. Before you meet

  • Review the companion's profile and booking details carefully.
  • Prefer a public or registered venue and arrange your own safe transport.
  • Tell a trusted person where you are going and when you expect to return.
  • Keep your phone charged and avoid sharing your home address or unnecessary personal information.
  • Do not send money outside the official payment flow or share passwords, OTPs or payment credentials.

5. During the meeting

  • Stay aware of your surroundings and trust your judgement.
  • Leave if you feel unsafe, pressured or uncomfortable.
  • Do not engage in illegal activity or pressure anyone to consume alcohol or drugs.
  • Respect privacy and do not secretly record or publish another member's private information.

6. Report, block and get help

Use the in-app reporting and blocking features to report harassment, suspicious conduct, unsafe behaviour or rule violations. Include accurate details where possible. Do not submit knowingly false reports.

If you use the in-app SOS feature, it is intended to notify BookAFriend's configured support channels. Delivery can be delayed or unavailable because of network, device or service-provider problems. It is not a substitute for contacting emergency services.

If you are in immediate danger in India, call 112. Move to a safer place if possible and contact a trusted person or the relevant authorities.

7. Prohibited conduct and enforcement

We may review reports and take action consistent with our Terms of Use, including requesting information, restricting features, suspending or removing accounts, or cooperating with lawful requests from authorities. Action may depend on the available evidence and applicable law.

8. Privacy and personal information

Share only information needed for the booking. Do not post another member's identity documents, contact details, precise location or private communications publicly. See our Privacy Policy for information about handling personal data.

9. Contact us

To report a safety concern or ask a policy question, visit Contact Us. For immediate emergencies, contact emergency services first.

These guidelines are general platform rules and safety advice, not a guarantee that every interaction will be safe. Have these guidelines reviewed for consistency with your actual product, operating procedures and applicable law before launch.
